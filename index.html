About This Tool
Welcome to the Reinforced Concrete Design (RCD) Tool — your interactive, student-friendly companion for mastering structural concrete design in accordance with the National Structural Code of the Philippines (NSCP 2015).

Built with a clean blue-themed interface and intuitive navigation, this tool transforms complex NSCP provisions into practical, step-by-step calculations. Whether analyzing singly-reinforced rectangular beams, T-beams, checking β₁ factors, φ strength reduction values, or shear and torsion design — every computation strictly follows NSCP 2015 (adopted from ACI 318M-14).
